# Acceptable Use Policy (AUP): Aether Edge AI
**Document ID:** POL-AUP-2026  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Effective Date:** March 20, 2026

---

## 1. Purpose
The purpose of this policy is to outline the acceptable use of information technology resources at Aether Edge. These rules are in place to protect the employee and the organization’s **$1.5M in PII assets** from risks such as data breaches and legal liability.

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third-party vendors who access Aether Edge networks, cloud environments (AWS/Azure), and AI modeling tools.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Data Protection & AI Ethics
* **Handling PII:** Users must not export or download PII (Personally Identifiable Information) to unauthorized personal devices.
* **AI Model Integrity:** Employees are prohibited from intentionally introducing biased or malicious data sets into Aether Edge AI models (**RS-1002**).

### 3.2 System Access & Passwords
* **MFA Requirement:** Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all corporate accounts without exception.
* **Prohibited Sharing:** Credential sharing is strictly prohibited; all access must be uniquely identifiable.

### 3.3 Remote Work & Security
* **VPN Usage:** All access to Aether Edge cloud storage and internal APIs (**RS-1003/1004**) must be conducted over an approved Corporate VPN.
* **Public Wi-Fi:** Employees are prohibited from accessing financial databases via unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

## 4. Unacceptable Use
Unacceptable use includes, but is not limited to:
* Circumventing security controls to access restricted financial data.
* Using corporate resources for the unauthorized mining of cryptocurrency.
* Engaging in any activity that compromises the **Availability** of Aether Edge services.

## 5. Compliance & Enforcement
Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment and legal action.
